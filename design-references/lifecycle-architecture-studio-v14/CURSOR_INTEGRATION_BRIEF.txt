VISUAL RULES
- No background grid.
- Dark graphite / charcoal base.
- Subtle granite/mineral texture burned into large dark surfaces.
- Muted copper structural accents.
- Desaturated teal technical/data accents.
- Soft ice accents only where useful.
- Warm off-white typography.
- Multicoloured architecture connectors/nodes may retain their semantic colours.
- Use subtle coloured separators between hero/main/side panels.
- Avoid excessive neon glow.
- Keep connector paths clean and deliberate; no lines crossing text or cards.

RESPONSIVENESS

SOURCE OF TRUTH
