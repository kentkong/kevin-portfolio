What the connectors mean

Every arrow represents a specific movement of customer data, an audience, a decision or an outbound experience. Shared rails show one platform distributing to several destinations without creating a tangle of independent lines.

Architecture principle

Centralise customer data, keep transformation reusable, add intelligence as a service, then activate through specialised engagement tools.

What role does CRM play?

CRM provides persistent customer and account context that other systems can enrich, analyse and activate.

Architecture roleSystem of record / operational customer context
Common dataProfiles, accounts, contacts, preferences, sales and service activity
Selection depends onB2B/B2C model, sales/service needs, ecosystem, scale and existing investment
Connects withWarehouses, marketing platforms, support systems, product data and BI
